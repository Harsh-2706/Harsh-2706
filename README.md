## About

I'm an AI/ML developer pursuing my B.Tech in Computer Science Engineering (AI & ML) at SRM Institute of Science and Technology, building intelligent systems and full-stack products that turn machine learning research into something people can actually use.

My work spans generative AI and applied ML — RAG pipelines, predictive models, context-optimization systems — to production engineering with FastAPI, ASP.NET Core, React, and Next.js. I care most about the space where research-grade AI meets clean, shippable software, and I've picked that up first-hand: shipping a tool that cuts LLM token costs by up to 92%, placing as a hackathon finalist, and interning across AI recruitment and B2B web development.
